Back turned? Tap the contraband to stuff it somewhere. Inspector looking? Freeze. Touching anything while watched is "tampering with evidence", and there is a form for that.

Don't touch the decoys. The microscope is very sensitive, the extinguisher is very full, and the intern is very asleep. Lab Crab wanders about on his own: hide him if you can catch him. The inspector won't write him up, but he will have opinions.

Shrimp Stacking!

The Golden Clipboard goes to the tallest professor in the ocean. The lab doesn't have a tall professor. It has three shrimp and one very long lab coat.

Tap to drop each scientist onto the one below. Stack nine to reach award height, then keep going: it gets faster with every shrimp. Miss and they fall off. Lean too far and the whole professor topples.

Watch out

Lab Crab lives in the coat pocket. When he climbs up onto the professor's head, wait for him to leave. Drop a shrimp on him and he pinches, and everything wobbles.
